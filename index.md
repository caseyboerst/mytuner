# Privacy Policy for Tuner

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Tuner app for Android (`com.caseycode.tuner`) handles your information.

## Summary

Tuner does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Microphone access

Tuner requests the microphone permission (`RECORD_AUDIO`) to detect the pitch of your instrument while you tune.

- Audio is processed in real time, in memory, on your device only.
- Audio is never recorded to a file, saved, or stored.
- Audio is never transmitted off your device. The app does not have internet access.

You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device's Settings. The tuner will not work without it.

## Data collection and sharing

Tuner does not:

- collect personal information,
- use analytics, advertising, or tracking services,
- include third-party SDKs that collect data,
- share or sell any data with third parties.

## Children's privacy

Tuner does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
